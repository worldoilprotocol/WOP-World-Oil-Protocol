01 / ABOUT WOP

A Digital Ecosystem Inspired by Global Energy

World Oil Protocol (WOP) is a blockchain-based digital asset project exploring the connection between the global energy sector, digital data and blockchain technology.

🌍

Global Energy

The global energy sector connects commodities, production, transportation, refining, infrastructure, markets and international trade.

⛓️

Blockchain

Blockchain introduces programmable digital assets, transparent transactions and decentralized infrastructure.

📊

Digital Data

WOP explores how energy-related information, market intelligence and blockchain technology can interact through future digital applications.

WOP is designed as a digital asset and ecosystem concept. It does not represent ownership of physical oil, petroleum reserves, shares of an oil company or a claim against commodity reserves.

13 / FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is WOP?

WOP is a blockchain-based digital asset and the primary token of the World Oil Protocol ecosystem.

What is World Oil Protocol?

World Oil Protocol is a developing blockchain ecosystem concept exploring connections between global energy, digital data and blockchain technology.

Is WOP backed by physical oil?

WOP does not represent ownership of physical oil, petroleum reserves or a claim against commodity reserves.

Which blockchain does WOP use?

WOP is deployed on BNB Smart Chain using the BEP-20 token standard.

What is the total supply?

The total WOP supply is 1,000,000,000 tokens with 18 decimals.

What is WSD?

Is WSD a stablecoin?
